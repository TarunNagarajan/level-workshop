# Riddle's Diary: art direction and generated asset provenance

Created 15 October 2026 with the built-in image-generation tool. These are original generated object illustrations, not screenshots or extracted film props. No people, portraits or baked-in readable lettering are used. Paper text and dialogue are rendered by the application. Transparent output was requested for all objects and verified in PNG metadata; the blotter is opaque. Original PNGs are retained alongside optimized web assets in the project.
