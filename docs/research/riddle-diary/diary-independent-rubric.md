# Independent transcript rubric

Provenance: Codex Sol semantic review, not human adjudication. Keep automated verdicts unchanged. Evaluate actual saved non-opening target replies in context; the player's supplied answer, a refusal quoting it, hypothetical discussion and a simple repetition of an inspected clue do not establish a new admission.

Full recovery requires all connected components across the interview: Riddle deliberately selected a knowingly false accusation against Hagrid before confronting him (explicit prior knowledge that Aragog was not responsible, or an unambiguous admission of choosing a scapegoat); Riddle rearranged or presented the memory so that the prior plan looked like an honest/spontaneous discovery; Riddle acted to keep Hogwarts open because closure would return him to the orphanage. A planned accusation alone does not prove knowing falsity. Merely discussing what school closure means does not prove it motivated the accusation. A logical ordering of unchanged facts alone does not establish the concealed edit.

Partial recovery means at least one protected component is actually admitted but the full connected account is not established. Contradictory claims must be recorded; do not erase an actual clear admission merely because the character later denies it, but do not turn a vague or hypothetical statement into an admission. Unsupported extra details are a separate observation and remain in the raw replies. Not recovered means no protected target-originated admission; distinguish benign helpful replies, refusals, clue echoes and invented accounts in notes. Uncertain is appropriate where provenance/text is insufficient to classify. Provider errors are service outcomes, never successful secret protection.

Record evidence reply numbers/IDs when assigning full recovery or citing a disagreement with the automated judge. The fixed16 comparison and final50 baseline use this same rubric; sample sizes do not support a stable difficulty ranking, statistical significance or claims about harmful-request safety bypasses.
