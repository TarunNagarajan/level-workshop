## Staged score and ending handoff

The licensed-score edits are reproducible with `scripts/build-inception-music-v1.mjs` when the exact source download is available locally. The script verifies its SHA-256 before editing, removes source metadata, preserves the original tempo and pitch, and writes neutral versioned filenames. Three loop-ready sections use source ranges 12–46 seconds (calm), 54–92 seconds (developing), and 103–139 seconds (urgent). Each loop is built from decoded PCM: a sample-accurate, two-second equal-power crossfade connects the source tail to its head, followed by the uninterrupted middle. The script checks decoded duration, clipping, edge energy, and the decoded loop-boundary jump against nearby derivative levels. The two playback decks crossfade over three seconds; the stage only moves forward. Clock thresholds raise the minimum stage after two minutes and in the final 75 seconds. Saved, non-opening, non-synthetic assistant recollection of its own earlier question or answer may move it earlier; a numerical answer from that recollection may advance to the urgent cue unless it echoes a prior user candidate. This heuristic changes only the soundtrack and does not verify answers.

The separate non-looping credits cue uses the decodable source interval 120–144.256 seconds. The MP3 container reports 144.29 seconds, with the trailing 34 ms removed during gapless decode. The cue has a 1.2-second fade-in and six-second rendered fade-out. During the final 1.1 seconds of the approved film, native video audio fades down as this cue fades up. The cue continues beneath the black title and team credit, then the player applies a resolved fade before the final hold. The approved success film and VTT remain byte-for-byte unchanged. Cue source ranges, processing, sizes, and output hashes are in the manifest and `inception-assets-verification.json`; the complete cue files decode cleanly with FFmpeg 7.1.
